1) our task
	- given text of product review, predict stars.
2) Dataset
	- Amazon Fine Food Reviews -> 568000 reviews
3) Our approach, does training with losses help the ordered star system
	- 3 types of losses
		a) CE -> normal loss and our baseline
		b) OLL -> Ordinal Log Loss -> further away rating, bigger loss
		c) MLL -> blend of CE and OLL
4) Model we use 
	a) textCNN -> smaller older network, uses GloVE, slides window of word to spot patterns
	b) DistillBERT -> large english language model -> we finetune it.
	
so ---- 2 models, 3 losses -> 6 settings, 3 random seeds per settings.

5) Success Evaluation
	a) Accuracy
	b) Macro F1 -> score that treats each star class equally
	c) MAE -> average number of stars we are off by 
	d) Large Error Rate -> the share of guesses off by 2 stars or more
	
6) SUCCESS RULE FROM PROPOSAL 
	- a loss counts as a win if it, compared with CE, 
		1) cuts MAE by atleast 3%
		2) cuts the large error rate by atleast 5%
		3) loses no more than 0.01 of the macroF1
		
		
7) PROGRESS SO FAR
		1) cleaned data (175000 copies)
		2) Split by product, not review (80/10/10)
		3) ran all 6 settings once (1 epoch) with seed 0.
		4) RESULTS -> TextCNN had all 3 sucess reached, DistillBERT not (but one run only, and its a bigger model, had only 100k reviews rn)
8) WHAT COMES NEX
		1) three seeds per settings
		2) tune settings with validation (lamba, OLL strength,learning rate, epochs)
		3) complete DistillBERT (more data and epochs)
